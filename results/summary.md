## 1. Vector add (sanity check + bandwidth)

Memory-bound: 1 add per 12 bytes moved, so the ceiling is HBM bandwidth. Both GPU variants sit at ~250-260 GB/s. Measured here, float4 is slightly SLOWER than scalar: at the bandwidth ceiling 128-bit loads buy nothing, which is itself the result.
